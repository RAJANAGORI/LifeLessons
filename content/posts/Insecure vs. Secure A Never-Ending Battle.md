---

It’s a perpetual war between these two mindsets. A secure and confident person radiates happiness. Such individuals not only cherish their own joy but also amplify yours. They exude positivity, handle situations with composure, and never create chaos—let alone return to fix what they themselves disrupted.  
  
On the other hand, insecure individuals are a different breed. They befriend you, initially admiring your qualities and believing they have much to learn from you. However, their insecurities soon morph into jealousy. They start imitating you, not out of admiration, but in an attempt to overshadow you. They project their struggles onto you, subtly making you feel like the root cause of their failures.  
  
The key? Maintain your distance. They are inauthentic, but you are not—and that is the defining difference.
