These words are self-explanatory: one reflects genuine commitment, and the other a fleeting phase rooted in selfishness. What are we becoming? Why are we becoming like this? Why aren’t we asking ourselves about the difference between the two?  
This dopamine dose for emotional attention, without acknowledging someone’s effort, is one of the saddest trends today. One person may be struggling internally, going out of their way to be kind and respectful, thinking the other deserves it, while the other shows—nothing.  
Human development, both mentally and emotionally, is similar to a mother giraffe kick her newborn’s ass after birth, ensuring it learns to stand. If the calf struggles, the giraffe keeps kicking the ass until it finds its footing.  
  
Embrace the essence of this example and respect genuine effort.
