At times, we clutter our lives with countless sticky notes, each one representing a task or commitment. This sudden surge of responsibilities can overwhelm us, leading to a profound sense of FOMO—Fear of Missing Out.  
  
This rapid escalation of duties not only breeds frustration and anger but also makes the ambitions we’ve nurtured feel increasingly distant from reality. It prompts a deeper reflection: Why do we allow ourselves to fall into this relentless cycle? Why do we feel trapped in the endless race, convinced we’re not progressing?
