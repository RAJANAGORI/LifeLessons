*The sunset spent the whole day proving itself, only to disappear without applause. The sunrise showed up empty-handed and still got treated like a fresh opportunity.*  
  
*By sunset, you realize not every battle was worth fighting, not every person was worth understanding, and not every plan deserved your loyalty. The sunset is a reminder that experience usually arrives after it would have been useful.*  
  
*Then the sunrise appears, carrying nothing but possibility. Hope, after all, has an impressive ability to ignore evidence.*  
  
*Life has a strange sense of humor. It rewards timing more than effort, confidence more than certainty, and sometimes the people who arrived late get credit for lessons you learned the hard way.*  
  
*Still, every sunset carries experience, every sunrise carries possibility, and somehow we spend our lives needing both. That's probably what growth looks like.*  
  
*Somewhere between the two, we call ourselves adults and continue pretending we know what we're doing.*
