There are moments when nothing seems to go as planned. Despite your utmost efforts and dedication, progress feels elusive, and failure appears imminent.

This is a natural part of the journey. It indicates that something may have gone awry or fallen short of expectations, warranting closer scrutiny and refinement.

If you sense that setbacks are inevitable and the mere thought of them gives you goosebumps, it suggests that such challenges may indeed arise at some point.

Allow yourself time to pause and reflect. Be kind to yourself and avoid harsh self-criticism.
