# A chaotic mind  
I often think a lot—about all the shit you’ve gone through, the work you do, the grind you did for yourself. How are you going to pick yourself up again when you realize everything could get messed up one day, and you have to start from scratch, back at zero?  
  
Are we in the mental state to do that? The smile on your parents’ faces, the anticipation from your brothers, hoping you’ll achieve something for them—all of that comes to mind in a second.  
  
You know what a real blessing is? Supportive parents, understanding cousins, a few office friends, and some chaddi buddies.  
  
I’ve heard a lot from others about their problems and messed-up situations, and when I have mine, I have a few people I believe will be happy in my good times and stand by me in my hard times.
