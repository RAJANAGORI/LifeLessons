There are days when ideas won’t come.  
There are days when things don’t go as planned.  
There are days when you do nothing, just sitting idle.  
There are days when you imagine success but aren’t in the zone to make it happen.  
There are days when your words unintentionally leave others speechless.  
There are days when you want to work or read a book, but nothing feels on track.  
There are days when you eat not because you love to, but simply because you have to.  
  
And here we are, making promises every New Year, only to end up clueless.  
Maybe it’s because of the internet, maybe the grass seems greener on the other side, or maybe it’s just a phase that will pass.
