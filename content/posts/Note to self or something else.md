```
Success isn’t some fairy tale. It’s a war. Everyone glorifies the victory, but nobody talks about the bloodbath it takes to get there. Dreams don’t hand themselves to you, they rip you open and demand everything: your sleep, your sanity, your soul.

You’ll be rejected so many times it’ll feel like the world wants you erased. You’ll lose people you thought were permanent. You’ll drown in failures so heavy they choke the air out of your chest. And every time you think you’re done, life will kick you harder, just to see if you’ll finally stay down.

The journey isn’t inspiring it’s savage. It’s waking up with nothing left but rage and still dragging yourself through the fire. It’s swallowing pain until it poisons you, then spitting it back out as fuel. It’s fighting tooth and nail when your body screams to quit, because you know quitting means you die with regret.

And when that dream finally breaks open, when all that suffering pays off it’s not about joy. It’s not about glory. It’s about standing on the ruins, breathing heavy, scarred, and snarling, knowing you survived what was built to destroy you.
That’s the real journey. It doesn’t make you a winner. It makes you a weapon.

```
