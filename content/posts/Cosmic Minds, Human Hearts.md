**FYI - Here “I” is a TPV (Third Person View) **  
  
People often place trust in timing, not merely in hours or dates, but in statements like *“we will meet,” “I’ll call,”* or *“it will be done in the meantime.”* and all it’s derivatives. Such expressions suggest certainty, yet their fulfillment depends on human capacity rather than intention alone. Waiting, therefore, becomes a common experience.  
  
Life does not move uniformly. Many individuals are navigating difficult phases, personal struggles, or mental overload. During such periods, communication may reduce or stop altogether. Some **choose silence**, others **delay responses**, and some **step away without explanation**. These situations usually create two possible interpretations.  
  
One interpretation turns inward. It assumes failure in roles, like friend, colleague, family member, partner, or any relational identity. Expectations are re-examined, and responsibility is questioned.  
  
The other interpretation looks outward. It recognizes that people may be **genuinely occupied**, **emotionally drained**, or prioritizing **survival** over **connection**. “Being busy” may function less as an excuse and more as a coping mechanism.  
  
This contrast reflects the difference between **cosmic minds** and **human hearts**.  
  
A cosmic mind seeks structure, patterns, and predictability. It expects cause and effect, effort and response, intention and outcome to align logically.  
  
A human heart operates differently. It is shaped by emotion, fatigue, fear, and inconsistency. It may miss commitments, postpone communication, or withdraw, not always out of disregard, but due to limitation.  
  
Understanding life requires acknowledging both perspectives simultaneously:  
    * Absence does not always indicate rejection.  
    * Silence does not always imply fault.  
    * Promises do not always withstand changing circumstances.  
  
Human connections are often **temporary**, **contextual**, and **reflective**. People enter lives to offer **perspective** rather than **quality of lasting**. The world functions less on balance and more on adaptability.  
  
Clarity, then, becomes essential not detachment, but awareness. Awareness to engage without **over-assuming**, to wait without **self-blame**, and to disengage when continuation demands excessive emotional cost.  
  
This balance between **expansive understanding** and **emotional realism** defines the interaction between **cosmic perception** and **human behavior**.  
It offers a grounded way to interpret life and people.
