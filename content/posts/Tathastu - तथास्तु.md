We often heard- कर्म करो, फल की चिंता मत करो ।  

We really need to understand this, you helped a person in their hard times, you gave a back support when nothing went right in their life, you were there when they needed you the most. Fine, they asked to you because they think from all the folks they have in their daily life won’t help them.  
Example, you helped some in finding a job, or you helped some in preparing for the interview. This is what your part of task that honestly you completed and you should accept the fact that this will never be the same when it comes to you.  
The one, whom you helped in reviewing the resumes. The one you saw them crying. The one, you listen all the shits happening in their life and yet telling them - ho jayega sab theek. All of them will never come to you when you need them the most and my understanding that’s OKAY. because once they started their life which they were struggling, is now more struggling when they got what they won’t and because of that they won’t there and of course some just take ADVANTAGES (iykyk😛)  
Now coming to title of this - why tathastu?   
You did what you think was right at that moment. You can’t see the people facing the same problem that you faced before and since you know the pain, you know how it is hard for someone as each and everyone’s handling the situations are different.   
  
Sooo, do it whenever you feel you can add a little help in their’s life and rest let handle by ☝🏻
