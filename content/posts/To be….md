What is happening to the world?
Someone is trying hard to contact you, and there is no reply.
Like a cold-blooded murderer.
Zero feelings for others.
Like a selfish human being.  

Then they show up like nothing happened.
Like your messages never sat there.
Like your waiting never happened.  

Social media is making us dumb, the same as AI.
You want to show engagement, but you don't want to show up for those who genuinely care for you.  

Complete ignorance.  

I think what people want is simply:  

To be heard, without screaming
To be missed, without leaving
To be noticed, without trying
To be understood, without explaining
To be chosen, without competition
To be safe, without hiding
To be enough, without proving
To be remembered, without reminding
To be trusted, without questioning
To be held, without asking
To be accepted without conditions
To be loved without reasons

But when you do absolutely shit to them, they want:

To scream to be heard
To leave to be missed
To try to be noticed
To explain to be understood
To compete to be chosen
To hide to be safe
To prove to be enough
To remind to be remembered
To question to be trusted
To ask to be held
To meet conditions to be accepted
To give reasons to be loved

I think they are tired.
Not physically, but mentally.
So they can't figure out what to do,
and they do absolutely nothing.

IN THE

TO BE, WITHOUT FEAR.
