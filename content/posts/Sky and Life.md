When we travel by air, we often see a quote on the back of the seat: *“PLEASE FASTEN SEAT BELT WHILE SEATED.”* One day, as my eyes lingered on this line, it struck me how perfectly it applies to life.  
  
The word *patience* comes to mind. Just like in the sky, where you have no control over the turbulence or the journey, life often demands patience. You don’t have an option but to stay calm and wait for the right moment to land safely.  
  
Similarly, when striving for something better—something truly worthwhile in life—you need patience. In today’s world of the internet, where we’re bombarded with instant gratification and hyped-up success stories of becoming a millionaire in 30 days, it’s easy to lose sight of what truly matters.  
  
We need to take a step back and reflect on the lessons from our parents—like your father, who worked tirelessly for years, navigating life’s chaos with grace, and providing you the best without ever comparing himself to others. There’s a profound beauty in his quiet dedication and love.  
  
It’s important to understand the difference between *caring with love* and *caring through comparison.* Love doesn’t measure or compete—it just gives, selflessly and patiently.
