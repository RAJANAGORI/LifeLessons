While reflecting on some of my past experiences—the things I was holding on to or the moments of sadness from not getting what I wanted—it all makes sense now.  
  
I’ve come to realize that the reason I didn’t achieve some of those things, despite wanting them so badly, is that they simply weren’t part of my destiny.  
  
Yes, yes, I know—this might sound like unsolicited advice or a cliché no one asked for, but honestly, I don’t care.  
  
As I’ve said before, whether it’s people who walked away from your life, exams you couldn’t pass, job interviews you didn’t crack, or even that bike you couldn’t buy—it could even be your biggest dream. If it didn’t happen at that moment in your life, it doesn’t mean it won’t ever happen. It might happen someday, or maybe something even bigger and better will come your way—something you couldn’t have imagined.  
  
The golden rule is simple: **Keep doing what you’re doing without asking anything from destiny.**
