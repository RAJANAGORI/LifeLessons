भ्रम, distraction, and manipulation. Powerful words, yet they’ll destroy you and your habits faster than you can blink.  
  
If you’re good at music, stop half-assing it and do it with everything you’ve got.  
If you’re good at drawing, stop being lazy and do it with passion.  
If you’re good at developing software that actually solves problems, put in the damn effort.  
  
Because let’s face it, aside from the few who genuinely respect your work, the rest of them are just waiting for you to fail.  
  
Here’s some truth you probably won’t like, but it is what it is:  
  
	•	If people like your work, they’ll praise you and give real feedback. But if they’re insecure as hell, they’ll say things like, “There’s no future in this,” “When will you finish?” or just throw out some random, illogical crap.  
  
	•	If they want to win an argument, they’ll start shouting to drown you out and shut you down.  
  
	•	They’ll brainwash you with meaningless nonsense to make you feel like you’re nothing—not because they’re confident, but because they’re just idiots full of hot air.  
  
	•	The majority will only bitch about problems, but the winners are the ones focused on solutions, not the ones who are stuck whining.  
  
You’ve got two choices:  
Either create an aura so strong that no one can spew their bullshit in front of you, or keep accepting their nonsense because you let them.
